A lightweight, **100% Free & Open-Source (FOSS)** camera app built for OnePlus devices running OxygenOS / ColorOS. Specifically engineered to **eliminate the aggressive AI skin-whitening, highlight blowout, and milky pale wash** while providing all top flagship camera features.

---

## 🌟 Top Flagship Features

* **🚫 Zero AI Skin-Whitening:** Forces `CaptureRequest.CONTROL_SCENE_MODE = DISABLED` and strips proprietary vendor beauty masks.
* **🎥 4K UHD Video Recording:** Record crisp 4K / 1080p video with high-bitrate audio and zero AI smoothing.
* **🎛️ PRO / Manual Mode:** Manual controls for ISO (100–3200), Shutter Speed, Focus, and Exposure Compensation.
* **🌙 Nightscape Mode:** Long-exposure night photography with natural shadow retention.
* **👤 Portrait Mode:** Natural background bokeh simulation with authentic human skin tones.
* **🔍 Multi-Lens Switching:** Instant hardware buttons for **0.6x (Ultra-Wide)**, **1.0x (Main 50MP Sony IMX890)**, **2.0x (Telephoto)**, and **5.0x (Zoom)**, plus continuous pinch-to-zoom (0.6x to 10.0x).
* **📐 Digital Horizon Leveler:** Real-time accelerometer leveler that turns green when the phone is held perfectly straight.
* **⏱️ Self-Timer & Grid:** 3s / 10s countdown with visual animation and Rule-of-Thirds composition grid.
* **☀️ Highlight-Protection Tuning:** Features a default -0.3 EV highlight-protection bias with live on-screen EV slider (-2.0 to +2.0 EV).
* **🛡️ 100% Bloat-Free & Offline:** **Zero `android.permission.INTERNET`**. No analytics, no ads, no cloud sync, under 17 MB.
* **📱 No Root Required:** Installs as a standalone APK on any OnePlus phone running Android 8.0+.

---

## 📥 Download & Installation

1. Download the latest APK from the [GitHub Actions Artifacts](https://github.com/Deekshith06/OnePlus-Natural-Cam/actions).
2. Tap the downloaded `.apk` file on your OnePlus phone.
3. Tap **Install** and grant Camera/Microphone permissions.
